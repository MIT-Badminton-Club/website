MIT Badminton Club

The MIT Badminton Club is a competitive club that competes in the USAB Eastern Collegiate Championships (Northern Conference) and local tournaments throughout New England. The club is open to MIT affiliates (students, faculty, staff, alum, or spouses of any of the above) with prior badminton experience. If you're new to the sport or just looking to play recreationally, we recommend open rec, intramural badminton, or taking a PE class instead.
